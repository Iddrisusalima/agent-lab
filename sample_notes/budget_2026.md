# Project Harbor - 2026 Budget

All figures are invented for this sample corpus and are stated in US dollars.
This file is the only place in the corpus where prices appear.

## Income

| Source | Amount |
|---|---|
| Regional science grant | 48000.00 |
| Community donations (2025 carry-over) | 3150.00 |
| **Total income** | **51150.00** |

## Unit prices

| Item | Unit price |
|---|---|
| Veridian TH-5 upgrade kit | 185.50 |
| Complete new station, parts only | 412.00 |
| Replacement Caldwell AN-2 anemometer | 64.75 |
| Replacement battery | 38.00 |
| Gateway receiver | 96.50 |

The upgrade kit price of 185.50 covers the sensor board, the heated humidity
element and the mounting hardware. It excludes volunteer time, which the
programme does not cost.

## Recurring costs

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cloud hosting, annual | 2340.00 |
| Per-station data and telemetry, annual | 37.20 |
| Insurance, annual | 1100.00 |
| Calibration reference instrument service, annual | 480.00 |

The per-station figure of 37.20 is charged for every active station in the
network. The station count itself is not recorded in this file; the per-region
counts and the network total are in `deployment_log.txt` and
`harbor_overview.md`.

## Planned spend

1. **Northern Lakes upgrade rollout.** One Veridian TH-5 upgrade kit for every
   station in the Northern Lakes region. The station count for that region is
   in `deployment_log.txt`; multiply it by the 185.50 unit price to get the
   rollout cost.
2. **Replacement pool.** Six anemometers, ten batteries and two gateway
   receivers held as spares.
3. **Four new stations** in the region chosen after the pending site survey.

## Spending rules

- Any single purchase above 2500.00 needs steering-group approval recorded in
  the meeting notes.
- The replacement pool is topped up once per quarter, never ad hoc.
- Volunteer travel is reimbursed at a flat 0.42 per kilometre, claimed monthly.
- Unspent grant money does not carry over; it returns to the funder at the end
  of the budget year.

## Reporting

The data editor publishes a one-page spend summary with the weekly archive on
the first publication of each month. The summary lists income, spend by
category and the remaining balance, and it never includes host addresses or
any volunteer's personal details.
