the only file that holds the per-region station counts.

REGION NOTES
------------
Northern Lakes    Oldest region. All 14 stations sit above 300 m and see the
                  worst autumn condensation, so this region is first in line
                  for the sensor-board upgrade kit.
River Delta       Largest region, 23 stations, mostly flat farmland. Radio
                  coverage is good; three stations share one gateway.
Cascade Foothills 17 stations spread over the longest driving distances in the
                  network. A full maintenance sweep here takes two days.
Harbor Flats      Newest region, 8 stations, commissioned during 2025.
